Project Overview

This project explores how healthcare spending in the United States has changed over time and examines the relationship between healthcare spending and broader economic growth.

The dashboard provides both a high-level snapshot of healthcare spending and more detailed views of:

* Nominal healthcare spending
* Real healthcare spending adjusted for inflation
* Healthcare price growth
* Healthcare spending growth
* Healthcare spending as a percentage of GDP
* GDP growth compared with healthcare spending growth
* Components contributing to healthcare spending growth

The primary dashboard view focuses on **Q4 2025**, while historical visualizations provide longer-term context.


Summary

The Summary page provides a high-level snapshot of healthcare spending as of Q4 2025.

Key metrics include:

* Annual healthcare spending
* Healthcare spending year-over-year growth
* Healthcare price growth
* Real healthcare spending growth, adjusted for inflation
* Healthcare spending as a percentage of GDP

A line chart compares the **Real Healthcare Spending Index, Healthcare Spending Index, and Price Index**, using 2017 as the base year.

Healthcare Spending Growth

This page examines healthcare spending growth over time.

A timeline compares:

* Nominal healthcare spending growth
* Real healthcare spending growth
* Healthcare price growth

This helps distinguish between increases in healthcare spending caused by higher prices and changes in the underlying volume of healthcare consumption.

GDP / Healthcare

This page examines healthcare spending in relation to the overall U.S. economy.

Visualizations include:

* Healthcare spending as a percentage of GDP over time
* Healthcare spending growth compared with GDP growth
* Healthcare spending's share of U.S. economic output

The dashboard highlights how healthcare spending has changed relative to broader economic growth.

### Waterfall Analysis

The Waterfall page breaks down healthcare spending growth into individual components.

The waterfall visualization is designed to show how the different components contribute to the overall change in healthcare spending.

Key Insights

The dashboard highlights several trends, including:

* Healthcare spending has grown over time in both nominal and inflation-adjusted terms.
* Healthcare price changes are an important component of changes in overall healthcare spending.
* Healthcare spending growth can differ from underlying real healthcare consumption growth.
* Healthcare spending represents a significant share of U.S. GDP.
* The dashboard's Q4 2025 analysis compares healthcare growth with broader economic growth to provide additional context.

One highlighted finding in the dashboard is that **healthcare spending as a percentage of GDP was 2.13 percentage points higher in 2025 than in 2001.**

Project Structure

The Power BI report contains four primary report pages:

1. **Summary** — High-level healthcare spending snapshot
2. **Healthcare Spending Growth** — Historical spending and price growth
3. **GDP / Healthcare** — Healthcare spending compared with GDP
4. **Waterfall** — Component-level growth analysis

Purpose

This project was created as a portfolio project to demonstrate the use of Power BI for transforming economic and healthcare data into an interactive, business-oriented reporting solution.
The goal was not simply to display data, but to present multiple perspectives that help users understand **what is driving healthcare spending growth and how healthcare spending compares with overall economic growth.**
